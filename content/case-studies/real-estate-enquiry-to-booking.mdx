In this example, a property developer receives an enquiry from a website form. Sales needs to follow up, Customer Service needs the booking details, and Accounts needs to verify what the buyer has paid.

## Assign the enquiry and schedule a visit

Bring the enquiry into the CRM and check whether the phone number already belongs to a lead. Assign the enquiry to a sales executive, record the buyer's preferences in the call notes, and schedule a property visit. The sales team can use its follow-up list to see when the next call is due.

## Check the unit and route the booking for approval

After the visit, check the status of the buyer's preferred unit. If it is available and the buyer wants to proceed, send the booking to the sales manager for approval. Once approved, hand the sale over to Customer Service so the team can continue from the booking record.

## Verify the first installment

Set up the installment schedule and record the buyer's first payment against it. Accounts verifies the payment before issuing a receipt. Customer Service can review the remaining balance and follow up when an installment is overdue.
