In this example, an admissions preparation team wants to run a timed mock exam that includes multiple-choice and written questions. Staff need to review written answers, and students need to know what to practise afterward.

## Build and schedule the exam

Organize questions in the question bank and use them to assemble the mock exam. Include multiple-choice questions for quick score feedback and written questions where students need to show their reasoning. Schedule the exam for the group.

## Take the timed exam

Students work through the questions while the timer runs. Answers autosave as they work. After the exam ends, staff can open written submissions and grade them.

## Review scores and plan the next practice session

Students can review their scores and see their progress by subject. A student who struggled with a topic can return to individual questions, read the multiple-choice explanations, and bookmark questions to revisit before the next mock exam.
