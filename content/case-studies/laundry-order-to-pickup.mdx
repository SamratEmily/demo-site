In this example, a customer drops off a bag of laundry and a jacket for urgent dry cleaning. The counter team needs to price the services differently, identify the order after processing, and check what the customer still owes.

## Price the order and tag the bag

Enter the laundry by weight and the jacket by piece. Apply the shop's configured urgent-service surcharge to the jacket. Print a numbered receipt for the customer and attach the scannable bag tag to the order.

## Mark the order ready

Use the order desk to mark the order ready once both services are complete. If the customer has a tracking link, they can check the status before coming to the shop.

## Find the order and settle the balance

When the customer arrives, scan the bag tag to pull up the matching order. Check the outstanding balance, record the payment, and mark the order as handed over. At closing, use the daily activity report to review the day's orders and payments.
