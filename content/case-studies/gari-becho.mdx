This product walkthrough follows a typical vehicle sale, from recording a purchase to settling with an investor.

## Record the purchase

When a vehicle arrives, record the supplier, purchase price, chassis number and engine number. If an investor helped fund the purchase, record their contribution so the shop can track it against the vehicle.

## Make the sale

Select the vehicle and buyer, then record the sale as cash or installments. For an installment sale, set up the payment schedule and print the sale memo.

## Follow up on installments

Open Due Alert to see which installments are due or overdue. Staff can use the list to decide who to call, then record each payment as it comes in.

## Review the investor settlement

Use the investor account to review the original contribution and the profit share calculated from the sale. Staff can work through the same records in Bangla or English.
