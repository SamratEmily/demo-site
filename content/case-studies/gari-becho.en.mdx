## The challenge

The shop kept its records in notebooks and on paper slips: which vehicle came from which supplier, who bought it, how many installments were left and which investor had funded it.

- Installment payments were missed because nobody had a single list of what was due each day.
- Working out the profit on each vehicle, and each investor's share, took hours at the end of the month.
- Only the owner knew the full picture, so the shop slowed down whenever they were away.

## Our solution

We set up [Gari Becho](/en/products/gari-becho/) for the shop and moved their existing records into it.

- **Stock and purchases:** every vehicle is recorded with its chassis and engine number the day it is bought.
- **Cash and installment sales:** staff choose the vehicle and buyer, set up installments and print the sale memo on the spot.
- **Due alert:** each morning the shop sees who has a payment due or overdue and calls them.
- **Investor accounts:** principal and profit share are calculated from the actual sales, so settlement is a report instead of a calculation.
- **Bangla and English:** staff use the language they are comfortable with.
