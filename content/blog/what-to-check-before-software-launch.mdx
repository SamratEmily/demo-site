A launch rehearsal is most useful when staff can work through a complete day, from the first transaction to the closing report. The following checks help you find problems that a feature-by-feature demo can miss.

## Test with the accounts people will actually use

Have a manager and a staff member sign in with their assigned roles. Ask them to enter an order, correct a mistake and find the right report. Check that each role can access the records it needs and that actions such as deleting records or viewing sensitive data are restricted to the appropriate people.

## Compare totals with a known example

Choose a day or reporting period with records you can reconcile. Compare opening balances, transactions and closing totals with the previous system. Include exceptions such as a cancelled sale or partial payment so the team can see how they appear in the reports.

## Rehearse recovery and support

Decide who can pause a rollout and what would trigger that decision. Restore a recent backup into a test environment and verify that it opens and contains the expected records. Give staff a clear way to report an issue and tell them what to include, such as the time, record reference and steps leading to the problem.

## Set up a follow-up after launch

Review the first day with the people doing the work. Keep a short list of issues, assign each one an owner and review the first complete reporting cycle. Once the core workflows are stable, use that list to decide which improvements should come next.
